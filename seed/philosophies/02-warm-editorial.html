Issue Nineteen · Michaelmas

A bindery in Devon

The paper keeps the hand that folded it.

Forty years at the bench have left Margaret Hale with a thumb that reads grain direction faster than any machine on the floor. She folds against it once, feels the fibres give, and knows which way the sheet wants to go. The book will open flat for two centuries because of a gesture that takes her under a second.
